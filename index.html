About This App & Content

This progressive web application (PWA) brings together the exhaustive, verse-by-verse breakdown, explanations of why Surahs were named, theological reflections, and comprehensive stories of the Quran as compiled from classical scholarship.

Content Integrity: All reflections, verse divisions, historical insights, and prophetic narratives have been preserved directly from the curated Quranic documentation without altering your source texts.

Section 508 & Accessibility: Designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 standards with high contrast ratios, semantic landmarks, full keyboard support, dynamic font sizing, and screen reader compatibility.

Offline Capable: Built with service worker caching so you can read, reflect, and learn anywhere—even without an active internet connection.
